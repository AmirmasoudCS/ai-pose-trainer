Camera tips
  • Squats: stand sideways (or at about 45°) so hips, knees and ankles are visible.
  • Push-ups: put the phone on the floor 2-3 m away and lie sideways to it, whole body in frame.
  • Keep your whole body in the picture, use even light and avoid a window behind you.
  • Prop the phone up so it does not move during the exercise.
